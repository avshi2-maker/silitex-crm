1. D4 / D5 / D6
• EU: D4 is already prohibited in cosmetics. Under REACH (Reg. (EU) 2024/1328), D5 is limited to 0.1% in rinse-off products (since 2020) and from 06/06/2027 also in leave-on products; D6 is limited to 0.1% in both from 06/06/2027.
• Israel: the cosmetics framework is modelled on the EU Cosmetics Regulation. Whether the REACH D5/D6 limits also apply to products sold only in Israel I am confirming with the Ministry of Health, and I will send you the official reference.
• In practice: every Israeli manufacturer exporting to the EU must meet the 2027 dates, so D5/D6-free alternatives are a real reason for R&D teams to test now.
