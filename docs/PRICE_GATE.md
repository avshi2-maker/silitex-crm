# The testing gate — "give me a better price first" · 13/10/2026
The objection, verbatim from 20 years in the field: *"To make me spend lab hours and money testing your silicone, show me a better price than the one I pay today."*

## Why it is rational (and why you can't argue it away)
- A plant's R&D has a validated process. Switching = re-validation (lab + pilot + QA + possibly customer re-approval). That costs them €2–10k per SKU in time before the first drum.
- For a *commodity* (dimethicone, D5, standard antifoam emulsion) the only variable left is price → the gate is correct and you will lose: Wacker / Shin-Etsu / Dow via local stockists have scale, local stock, and no Italian freight.
- Freight from Italy: a 1-t pallet of 10% antifoam emulsion is 90% water. At €2–3/kg product value, freight+duty is 15–25% of landed cost. Nobody beats a local commodity on this.

## Where the gate does NOT apply (sell only here)
1. **Problem, not price.** The plant has foam it cannot kill, an MBR membrane it fouls, a filling line that overflows, a pH-14 CIP that eats the antifoam. Test motivation = fixing a problem. Silitex has the depth here (DYNAKOS, SUREVAT, CPL 2907, ALKAFLUX).
2. **Certificate, not price.** Kosher drum (SILIFOOD 30, CPL 350 ET, CPL 250 FOOD), FDA direct food contact, Ecolabel / 100% vegetal (FISIOREX, POLIVEG, CPL 330 ORGANIC), Pharma-grade dimethicone (Ph. Eur./USP). The certificate is specified by the customer's customer or by the rabbinate — R&D must test.
3. **Regulation, not price.** EU 2027 cyclics (D4/D5/D6) restriction forces every cosmetics exporter to reformulate. A forced reformulation IS the testing window — and Silitex has the D5-free path (DIMETIBLEND, ALKABLEND, alkanes, DIMETHICONE 1.5 cSt, BODYAMISIL). Sell into the reformulation, not against the current SKU.
4. **New project, not replacement.** A new product line has no incumbent. Be in the formulator's hands at the brief stage (the Market Engine exists for this).
5. **Cost per batch, not per kg.** A formulated antifoam at 0.1% dose vs a cheaper one at 0.3% is cheaper per batch. Quote per 1,000 L treated, never per kg. Needs Silitex dosage data per application (the catalogues give ranges).
6. **Supply risk, not price.** Dual-sourcing policy after 2021–24 shortages: procurement wants a second qualified supplier. That is a test with no price argument — but it needs stock in Israel and a 2-year price commitment.

## What to ask Silitex for (the four things that convert tests)
1. **Sample policy** — up to 25 kg free, shipped within 10 days, with the dosage protocol for the application.
2. **Trial-lot pricing** — first 500 kg at distributor cost +10%, to get through the first production run.
3. **Dosage / performance data per application** from their lab (the catalogues have ranges; need the curve for the "cost per batch" argument).
4. **Technical answer SLA** — 60 days max on any R&D question; a named lab contact.
Plus, medium term: consignment or distributor stock in Israel for the 10 fastest SKUs (freight becomes invisible, lead time becomes days).
